# A uniform gap below two for triangle packings and edge transversals in K₄-free graphs of book size at most three

**Written research record · 20 November 2026 (Asia/Shanghai).** This statement records the manuscript version held by the depositor, **denudatamour**.

For every finite simple graph $G$ with no $K_4$ in which each edge belongs to at most three triangles, the manuscript proves

$$177\,\tau_\triangle(G)\le 353\,\nu_\triangle(G).$$

Here $\tau_\triangle(G)$ is the minimum number of graph edges meeting every triangle, and $\nu_\triangle(G)$ is the maximum number of pairwise edge-disjoint triangles. The argument also applies to any specified subfamily of triangles. The manuscript has undergone internal AI-assisted proof checks and awaits external review.
