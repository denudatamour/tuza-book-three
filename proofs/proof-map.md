# Proof outline

The theorem applies to any triangle family in a finite simple K4-free graph whose edges belong to at most three triangles.

| Section | Argument |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The triangle conflict graph turns edge covers into clique covers. |
| 2 | A ten-profile potential gives a packing bound by closed-neighborhood induction. |
| 3 | A random edge cover is improved by simultaneous matching exchanges. |
| 4 | A local packing bound follows from five-wheel labels. |
| 5 | Retaining unmatched faces and combining the bounds gives 177 tau <= 353 nu. |

The five-wheel step uses Munaro's result cited in the paper. The arithmetic scripts verify the finite tables and constants; the remaining arguments are in the written proof.
